Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit the Sir Gentleman Barbershop website and how it is used.

Information we collect

This website has no contact forms, accounts, or online payments, and we do not ask you to enter personal information on it. If you call, text, or message us, we use what you share only to answer you and provide our services. We do not sell your information.

Third-party services on this site

  • Google Maps: the map on our home page is loaded from Google. When it loads, Google receives your IP address and basic browser information.
  • Google Fonts: our text styles are loaded from Google, which receives the same kind of information.
  • Social media: we link to our Instagram page. Instagram has its own privacy policy.
  • Our website host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, pages visited, and time of visit, for security and to keep the site running.

These companies handle that information under their own privacy policies. You can read Google's at policies.google.com/privacy.

Cookies

We do not set cookies. The embedded Google map may set its own cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
